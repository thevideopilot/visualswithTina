About

Tina Awodola

United Kingdom

I'm a UK-based videographer specialising in beauty and lifestyle content. I create films that feel intimate, intentional, and visually rich, the kind that stop the scroll and move product.

From studio shoots and on-set commercials to skincare launches, PR campaigns, and lifestyle content, I bring the same cinematic eye to every brief. Every brand has a world worth stepping into. My job is to find it, light it beautifully, and put it on screen.
